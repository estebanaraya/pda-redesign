PDA Redesign

0.4.0-alpha · public alpha for testing · 2026-11-05

Every tier and every function, shown. Frames are 4K test-run captures from 2026-11-05, most on the PBR build. Public alpha for testing: expect rough edges, and report what you find at github.com/estebanaraya/pda-redesign/issues.

Quality of life

Inventory and search

  • Search as the last Sort tab; in stashes one field searches both panes
  • Type filters on the backpack
  • Group by type: categories fold like an accordion, while looting too
  • Group stashes by type (Settings, Inventory, Backpack; off by default)
  • Move all and Take all also take folded items
  • Total carry weight under every Sort tab
  • Right-click works on every box
  • Drop onto equipment: a module on the Promin, a filter or tank on the helmet or Air box, a scope on a scoped weapon (they swap), an item on an empty Belt box; with SOTA a consumable on Ready box N sets FN
  • Refusal hints when a drop does not fit
  • Equipment pane with the WD slots (Forearm, Wrist, Bracer, Patch, Side), hatched without a bracer
  • Weapon chips: magazines (Mags Reloaded) and fitted addons on the weapon boxes
  • Virtual sidearm box; with two primaries the knife key twice draws the sidearm
  • Chest rig: plate in the rig off the Belt, med pockets; SOTA rigs, pouches and Ready supplies in our look
  • Item details: floating two-column sheet, Attachments block, upgrade tree, a rig's pocket layout; no hover card over it
  • Visor generation on the helmet's AR chip; binocular tier badge on the cell, card and upgrade screen
  • Trade sets apart what the trader will not buy
  • See-through AR glass or Flat; Backing darkness setting; the HUD dims behind the inventory

Crafting and bench

  • One grid for every workshop mode, Available only, prominent search
  • Bench upgrade trees with path lines and kit art
  • Our recipes take the artifact in an IAM, AAC or AAM; no money

PDA and HUD

  • Map: filters, pan, zoom, Centre on player, right-click menu; zoom and pan stop at the world edge
  • Level exits as chevrons; clicking a spot on a preview opens the Map on it
  • Every setting in the PDA's Settings, MCM and the HUD editor; every key on Controls
  • Presets: Default, PROMIN Hardcore, Unrestricted
  • Quick slots fade 4 s after use, or Always shown
  • Skip what devices show: nothing a lit Promin page shows is drawn twice
  • Better Beef's NVGs bloom held at 0 while the PDA is up
  • Talk window and use prompt in the PROMIN look; GFY faces
  • Prepare for uninstall: one press readies a save

Setup

Needs

  • G.A.M.M.A. (installer 920), WD 0.9.2+, DX10 or DX11.
  • PBR (maka-pbr-test) or Modded Exes, shader bus 7: everything runs, the bus 8 effects fall back. PR 805 (bus v8): everything, on DX10/DX11 with MSAA off; the DX11 rows need DX11.
  • Recommended: Classic Binoculars Remodel (the FOB-45's motions) and Gryf's S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 Binoculars Remodel (the shared icon).
  • Install Z_PDA_Redesign low in MO2, below G.A.M.M.A.'s UI mods.

In the works: thermals, the wave's bodies and ring, the HUD's light and lens. Parked: visor cracks, fog, rain glow, the weapons HUD.

Known issues

  • Performance (budget 0.2 ms): 1080p world about +0.8 ms, map +0.3 ms, inventory within. Thermal and wave about +0.3 ms at 4K.
  • Binocular zoom: wheel steps zoom only while raised; the view and lens are still changing.
  • Parked: visor cracks, fog, rain glow (engine stage); no weapons HUD.
  • PBR: one native crash at a wave test step, untraced.
  • Gen 4 name tag: at Gen 4 the marks draw on everyone in view, but the name tag of the person under the crosshair may not draw (it does at Gen 2 and Gen 3). Under investigation.
  • Resolutions: checked mostly at 4K.
  • Russian: machine-written, unreviewed.
  • Notifications switch on the next load.
  • Uninstall: Prepare for uninstall first; see the README.
  • Report what you find: github.com/estebanaraya/pda-redesign/issues
